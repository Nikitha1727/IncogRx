Remove
Names, relatives, phones, Aadhaar, ABHA and street addresses, in English, हिन्दी and Hinglish.

        
Pseudonymise
Hospital IDs become one keyed pseudonym per patient, the same in every file.

        
Generalise
Age to a 10-year band, street address to district, a rare condition to its ICD chapter.

        
Shift dates
One secret shift per patient. The 4-day stay stays 4 days.
