Handwriting and scans
Read by OCR, checked by confidence

          

          
Pages without a text layer are read offline with Tesseract (English and Hindi models, a few MB, no AI service). Words the engine removes are blacked out in the image itself, the pixels underneath are deleted, and the copy is rebuilt as a new PDF. Words the OCR is unsure of are blacked out too, and pages with low confidence are flagged for a human check: when in doubt, IncogRx hides rather than leaks.

Privacy pipeline

            

              
              
              
              
            

            
Scan every upload for malware and active content, then read every supported file: PDFs page by page, scans and handwriting by OCR. Anything broken or unsafe is quarantined or blocked, never copied.
