03How it works

Four moves.No guesswork.

Rules and patterns only, no language model. Every move is keyed by a release secret, so the same input always gives the same release, and a new secret changes every pseudonym and date.

Move 01
Remove

Names, relatives, phones, Aadhaar, ABHA and street addresses, in English, हिन्दी and Hinglish.

↘
PSN-R10M7K
Move 02
Pseudonymise

One keyed pseudonym per patient, the same in every file, script and language.

↘
52 → 50-59
Move 03
Generalise

Age to a 10-year band, street address to district, a rare condition to its ICD chapter.

↘
4 days = 4 days
Move 04
Shift dates

One secret shift per patient, so admissions, stays and follow-ups keep their exact spacing.

↘
